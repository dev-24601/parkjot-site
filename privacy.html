Privacy Policy for ParkJot

Last updated: November 1, 2026

ParkJot respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

ParkJot does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. ParkJot does not require you to create an account or provide personal information.

Parking Notes and App Data

Parking notes and related app data are stored locally on your device.

ParkJot uses Apple's App Group storage to allow the ParkJot app and its widget to access shared parking information on your device. This information is not transmitted to our servers.

Analytics and Advertising

ParkJot does not use third-party analytics services and does not display advertisements.

Tracking

ParkJot does not track you across apps or websites.

Data Sharing

ParkJot does not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Data Security

ParkJot does not operate a server for storing your parking notes or related app data. Your app data remains under the control of your device and Apple's operating system.

Children's Privacy

ParkJot does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If ParkJot's privacy practices change in the future, including through the introduction of features or services that affect how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.
